# Functional English - I: Syllabus Coverage

## Course Objectives

This book develops proficiency in grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure and functional language. It strengthens academic, email and essay writing; improves reading, listening and comprehension; and builds confident communication for academic and professional situations.

## Course Outcomes

After completing the book, a learner should be able to:

1. Use English more accurately in reading, writing, speaking and listening.
2. Communicate ideas clearly and improve linguistic competence.
3. Apply practical communication skills in academic and professional settings.
4. Continue learning language independently throughout life.
