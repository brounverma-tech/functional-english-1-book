# Appendix G: A-Z Glossary

| Letter | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | Academic writing | formal, organised and evidence-based writing |
| B | Barrier | something that blocks communication |
| C | Clause | group of words containing a subject and verb |
| C | Coherence | logical flow and connection of ideas |
| C | Collocation | words that naturally occur together |
| D | Determiner | word placed before a noun to limit or identify it |
| E | Essay | structured writing that develops a central idea |
| F | Figurative meaning | meaning different from the literal words |
| G | Gist | main or general meaning |
| H | Hearing | physical reception of sound |
| I | Idiom | fixed expression with a special figurative meaning |
| I | Inference | conclusion drawn from evidence and context |
| I | Intonation | rise and fall of voice pitch |
| J | Judgement | conclusion or opinion formed after consideration |
| K | Keyword | important word carrying central information |
| L | Listening | active understanding of spoken information |
| M | Modal verb | helping verb expressing attitude or function |
| N | Narrative | account of connected events |
| O | Objective | intended learning result or purpose |
| P | Paraphrase | source idea expressed in genuinely new wording |
| P | Plagiarism | presenting another's work as one's own |
| P | Predicate | part of a sentence saying something about the subject |
| Q | Question tag | short question added to a statement |
| R | Register | level of language suited to a context |
| S | Sentence stress | emphasis placed on important words in speech |
| S | Soft power | influence created through attraction and culture |
| S | Subject | person or thing the sentence is about |
| S | Summary | brief statement of central ideas |
| T | Tense | verb form locating action in time and aspect |
| T | Thesis statement | sentence expressing an essay's central position |
| T | Tone | speaker's or writer's attitude |
| U | Unity | focus of a paragraph on one central idea |
| V | Verb | word expressing action, state or possession |
| V | Vocabulary | words known and used by a person |
| W | Word family | related words formed from a common base |
| X | eXpression | phrase used for a particular communicative purpose |
| Y | Yes-no question | question normally answered with yes or no |
| Z | Zero article | absence of a/an/the before a general noun |
