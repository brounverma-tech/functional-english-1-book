# Appendix B: Writing Templates

## B.1 Formal Email

**Subject:** [Specific purpose]

Dear [Title and Name],

I am writing to [state purpose directly].

[Give essential background/details in two or three short sentences.]

Could you please [state the required action]? I would be grateful if [polite condition or deadline].

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Regards,  
[Full Name]  
[Course / Roll Number, if relevant]

## B.2 Email for Leave

**Subject:** Leave Request for [Date/Period]

Dear Sir/Ma'am,

I am writing to request leave from [start date] to [end date] because [brief reason]. I will complete the missed work and collect the class notes after returning.

Kindly grant me leave for this period.

Regards,  
[Name and class]

## B.3 Academic Paragraph Frame

1. **Topic sentence:** [Main claim]
2. **Explanation:** [What the claim means]
3. **Evidence/example:** [Specific support]
4. **Analysis:** [Why the support matters]
5. **Closing link:** [Connection to central topic]

## B.4 Five-Paragraph Essay Plan

### Introduction

- Context/hook
- Narrow topic
- Thesis with main position

### Body Paragraph 1

- First reason
- Explanation
- Evidence/example

### Body Paragraph 2

- Second reason
- Explanation
- Evidence/example

### Body Paragraph 3

- Third reason or counterargument
- Response/evidence

### Conclusion

- Restated thesis
- Main points in brief
- Final implication/recommendation

## B.6 Film or Book Review

- Title and creator
- Genre/context
- Brief, spoiler-light overview
- Strengths with examples
- Limitations with examples
- Theme or significance
- Clear recommendation for a specific audience
