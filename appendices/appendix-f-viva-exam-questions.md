# Appendix F: Viva and Exam Questions

## F.1 Very Short Questions

1. What is Functional English?
2. What is a synonym?
3. Define sentence structure.
4. What is tense?
5. Name four modal verbs.
6. What is an abstract noun?
7. What is a topic sentence?
8. Define plagiarism.
9. What is a collocation?
10. Distinguish hearing and listening.
11. What is an idiom?
12. What does “break the ice” mean?

## F.2 Short-Answer Questions

1. Explain the four aspects of tense with examples.
2. Differentiate must not and do not have to.
3. Explain the eight parts of speech.
4. List the stages of the writing process.
5. Describe the structure of a formal email.
6. Why is Grandma Moses called an artist of hope?
7. How did freedom influence Tagore's creativity?
8. Give three educational benefits of music.
9. How does cinema support cultural exchange?
10. Explain the symbolism of the star in Chapter 10.
11. List the key features of a short story.
12. Why is Josh's judgment of Fred unreliable?
13. Explain active listening.
14. Give five polite conversation expressions.

## F.3 Long-Answer Questions

1. Discuss tense forms and their contextual uses with suitable examples.
2. Explain academic paragraph and essay organisation.
3. Analyse hope, adaptation and late achievement in “Grandma Moses.”
4. Discuss Tagore as a model of multidimensional creativity.
5. Evaluate the argument for music education.
6. Explain cinema as cultural exchange between India and Japan.
7. Discuss meaningful living and compassion in “Look for the Stars in Your Life.”
8. Trace the development and power of the short story form.
9. Analyse plot, prejudice and character change in “Judge Ye Not.”
10. Explain barriers to listening and strategies for overcoming them.

## F.4 Practical Writing Questions

1. Write an email requesting permission to attend a workshop.
2. Write an academic paragraph on digital communication.
3. Write an essay on “Communication Skills and Employability.”
4. Summarise one prescribed reading lesson in 150 words.
5. Prepare a listening note from a teacher's announcement.
6. Write a dialogue showing polite disagreement.

## F.5 Viva Answer Tips

- Start with a direct definition.
- Give one correct example.
- Use short and complete sentences.
- If comparing, explain both sides.
- Do not memorise without understanding.
- Ask politely if the question is unclear.
