# Appendix D: Listening Practice Scripts

Ask a partner or text-to-speech tool to read each script. Do not look at the text during the first listening.

## D.1 Announcement

> Attention students. The library will close at 4 p.m. this Friday for maintenance. Books due on Friday may be returned on Monday without a late fee. The digital library will remain available throughout the weekend.

Questions:

1. Why will the library close early?
2. What time will it close?
3. When can due books be returned without a fee?
4. Which service remains available?

**Answers:** maintenance; 4 p.m.; Monday; digital library.

## D.2 Telephone Message

> Hello, this is Meera from the placement office. Your interview practice session has moved from Wednesday morning to Thursday afternoon at 2:30. The room remains Seminar Hall B. Please bring two printed copies of your résumé and arrive fifteen minutes early.

Create a note containing: caller, new day, time, location, items and reporting time.

**Answer:** Meera, placement office; Thursday; 2:30 p.m.; Seminar Hall B; two résumé copies; arrive at 2:15 p.m.

## D.3 Short Talk

> A useful study plan is realistic rather than perfect. Begin by listing fixed commitments such as classes, meals and travel. Next, place difficult tasks during your most alert hours. Divide large assignments into smaller actions and include short breaks. At the end of each day, review the plan and move unfinished work without blaming yourself. Consistency matters more than an occasional very long study session.

Tasks:

1. State the main idea in one sentence.
2. List the four planning suggestions.
3. What matters more than rare long sessions?

**Possible answer:** A realistic, adjustable plan improves consistent study. List commitments; schedule hard tasks at alert times; divide assignments; review and adjust. Consistency matters more.

## D.4 Conversation

> A: I thought the presentation was due tomorrow.  
> B: The slides are due tomorrow, but the presentation is on Monday.  
> A: Thanks. Should I upload a PDF or PowerPoint file?  
> B: The teacher asked for a PDF. The file name should include our group number.  
> A: I will send it after everyone checks the final version.

Questions:

1. What is due tomorrow?
2. When is the presentation?
3. Which file format is required?
4. What must the file name contain?

**Answers:** slides; Monday; PDF; group number.

## D.5 Self-Evaluation

After practice, rate yourself from 1 to 5:

- I understood the main idea.
- I caught names, dates and numbers.
- I ignored unfamiliar minor words.
- I used short, accurate notes.
- I could summarise without reading the script.
