|---|---|

| better late than never | late action is better than none |
| by the book | according to rules |
| cut corners | do something cheaply or carelessly |
| get out of hand | become uncontrollable |
| in the long run | over a long period |
| keep your word | do what you promised |
| make up your mind | decide |
| no pain, no gain | effort is needed for progress |
| out of the blue | unexpectedly |
| put yourself in someone's shoes | imagine another person's situation |
| read between the lines | understand an implied meaning |
| take it with a pinch of salt | remain doubtful about a claim |
| the tip of the iceberg | small visible part of a larger issue |
| through thick and thin | during good and difficult times |

## E.5 Practice

Choose the natural collocation:

1. ___ a decision: do / make / build
2. ___ attention: pay / spend / create
3. ___ a deadline: meet / catch / touch
4. ___ rain: strong / heavy / powerful
5. ___ research: conduct / play / performate

**Answers:** make, pay, meet, heavy, conduct

Use any five idioms in original sentences. Check meaning, grammar and level of formality before using them.
