# Chapter 1: Vocabulary, Daily Routine and Sentence Structure

## 1.1 Learning Objectives

After this chapter, you will be able to:

- build useful everyday vocabulary;
- describe a daily routine in correct sequence;
- identify subject, verb and object;
- create affirmative, negative and interrogative sentences.

## 1.2 Key Words

| Word | Simple meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary | words known and used by a person | Reading improves vocabulary. |
| Routine | activities done regularly | Exercise is part of my routine. |
| Subject | person or thing doing the action | **Riya** reads. |
| Predicate | information about the subject | Riya **reads every day**. |
| Synonym | word with a similar meaning | begin - start |
| Antonym | word with an opposite meaning | early - late |

## 1.3 Vocabulary Building

**Vocabulary** is the collection of words we understand and use. Sirf difficult words yaad karna vocabulary building nahi hai. Word ka meaning, pronunciation, form, common partner word aur sentence mein use samajhna zaroori hai.

### 1.3.1 Word Family

Ek root se related words ban sakte hain:

- act (verb)
- action (noun)
- active (adjective)
- actively (adverb)
- activity (noun)

### 1.3.2 Synonyms and Antonyms

| Word | Synonym | Antonym |
|---|---|---|
| happy | glad, cheerful | sad |
| quick | rapid, fast | slow |
| accept | receive | reject |
| polite | courteous | rude |
| improve | develop | worsen |

Synonyms ka meaning similar hota hai, but har context mein words interchange nahi hote. For example, **big mistake** natural hai; **large mistake** grammatically possible lag sakta hai but common English nahi hai.

### 1.3.3 Five-Step Vocabulary Method

1. Word ko context mein read karo.
2. Meaning aur pronunciation check karo.
3. Word family note karo.
4. Apna original sentence banao.
5. 24 hours aur one week baad revise karo.

## 1.4 Daily Routine

Daily routine describe karte samay mostly **simple present tense** use hota hai.

> I wake up at 6 a.m. I brush my teeth and take a shower. After breakfast, I attend my classes. In the evening, I revise my notes. I go to bed at 10:30 p.m.

Useful sequence words: **first, then, after that, later, in the evening, finally**.

### 1.4.1 Useful Routine Expressions

- wake up / get up
- brush my teeth
- get dressed
- have breakfast
- leave for college
- attend lectures
- return home
- complete assignments
- spend time with family
- go to bed

**Hinglish tip:** Hindi sentence order ko word-by-word translate mat karo. “Main 7 baje college ke liye nikalta hoon” becomes **I leave for college at 7**, not “I for college at 7 leave.”

## 1.5 Sentence Structure

### 1.5.1 Basic Pattern: Subject + Verb + Object

> Neha reads books.

- Subject: Neha
- Verb: reads
- Object: books

Other common patterns:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| S + V | Birds fly. |
| S + V + O | Aman plays cricket. |
| S + linking verb + complement | The room is quiet. |
| S + V + indirect object + direct object | She gave me a pen. |

### 1.5.2 Four Sentence Types

1. **Declarative:** She studies English.
2. **Interrogative:** Does she study English?
3. **Imperative:** Study this chapter carefully.
4. **Exclamatory:** What a useful lesson this is!

### 1.5.3 Affirmative, Negative and Question

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | They attend class. |
| Negative | They do not attend class. |
| Question | Do they attend class? |

Third-person singular mein **does/does not** ke baad main verb base form mein aata hai: **Does he play?**, not “Does he plays?”

## 1.6 Common Mistakes

- ❌ He go to college. → ✅ He goes to college.
- ❌ I am having two brothers. → ✅ I have two brothers.
- ❌ She does not likes tea. → ✅ She does not like tea.
- ❌ Myself Rohan. → ✅ My name is Rohan. / I am Rohan.
- ❌ I wake up on 6 a.m. → ✅ I wake up at 6 a.m.

## 1.7 Practice

2. breakfast / I / at / have / eight
3. the / students / assignment / completed

### B. Fill in the blanks

1. Rahul ___ (go/goes) to college by bus.
2. I ___ not watch television in the morning.
3. ___ she read the newspaper daily?

### C. Writing Task

Write 100 words about your weekday routine. Use at least five sequence words.

## 1.8 Quick Revision

- Vocabulary grows through context and repeated use.
- Daily habits normally use the simple present tense.
- Basic English order is Subject + Verb + Object.
- **Do/does** helps form negatives and questions in simple present.

## 1.9 MCQs

1. Which is a synonym of “begin”?  
   a) finish  b) start  c) refuse  d) close
2. Choose the correct sentence:  
   a) He go daily.  b) He goes daily.  c) He going daily.  d) He does goes daily.
3. The subject in “Meera writes emails” is:  
   a) writes  b) emails  c) Meera  d) writes emails

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-c
