# Chapter 4: Parts of Speech

## 4.1 Learning Objectives

- identify the eight traditional parts of speech;
- understand how a word's function changes by context;
- use correct word forms in sentences;
- improve grammatical accuracy.

## 4.2 Why Parts of Speech Matter

Parts of speech words ko unke **job** ke according classify karte hain. Same word different sentences mein different jobs kar sakta hai.

- I drink **water**. (noun)
- Please **water** the plant. (verb)

Isliye word ka label sirf dictionary se nahi, sentence mein uske function se decide hota hai.

## 4.3 The Eight Parts

### 4.3.1 Noun

A noun names a person, place, thing, idea or quality.

- Proper: India, Riya
- Common: city, student
- Collective: team, class
- Abstract: honesty, freedom
- Material: gold, water

Countable: book/books. Uncountable: information, advice, furniture.

### 4.3.2 Pronoun

A pronoun replaces a noun.

- Personal: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- Possessive: mine, yours, hers
- Reflexive: myself, themselves
- Demonstrative: this, those
- Relative: who, which, that
- Indefinite: someone, many, none

Pronoun reference clear hona chahiye: “When Riya met Neha, **she** smiled” ambiguous hai because **she** could mean either person.

### 4.3.3 Verb

A verb shows action, state or possession.

- action: run, write
- state: know, seem
- helping: is, have, do
- modal: can, should

### 4.3.4 Adjective

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun: **a clear answer**, **three books**, **this class**.

Usual adjective order: opinion + size + age + colour + origin + material + noun.

> a beautiful small old brown Indian wooden table

### 4.3.5 Adverb

An adverb modifies a verb, adjective, another adverb or the whole sentence.

- She spoke **clearly**. (manner)
- He arrived **yesterday**. (time)
- They live **nearby**. (place)
- It is **very** useful. (degree)

Not every adverb ends in **-ly**: fast, hard, often. Some **-ly** words are adjectives: friendly, lovely.

### 4.3.6 Preposition

A preposition shows relationship of time, place, direction or method.

- at 5 p.m., on Monday, in August
- at the gate, on the table, in the room
- go to college, travel by bus, write with a pen

### 4.3.7 Conjunction

A conjunction joins words or clauses.

- Coordinating: and, but, or, so, yet, for, nor
- Subordinating: because, although, if, when, while
- Correlative: either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also

### 4.3.8 Interjection

An interjection expresses sudden feeling: **Oh! Wow! Alas! Hurrah!** It is often separated by punctuation.

## 4.4 Determiners and Articles

Modern grammar often treats determiners separately. They come before nouns: **a book, the answer, my class, some water, each student**.

- **a/an**: one non-specific singular countable noun
- **the**: specific or already known noun
- zero article: general plural or uncountable idea - “Books are useful.”

## 4.5 Word Formation

| Noun | Verb | Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|---|---|
| success | succeed | successful | successfully |
| decision | decide | decisive | decisively |
| beauty | beautify | beautiful | beautifully |
| communication | communicate | communicative | communicatively |

## 4.6 Common Mistakes

- ❌ She gave me an advice. → ✅ She gave me some advice.
- ❌ He speaks English good. → ✅ He speaks English well.
- ❌ Discuss about the issue. → ✅ Discuss the issue.
- ❌ I reached at college. → ✅ I reached college.
- ❌ She is more smarter. → ✅ She is smarter.

## 4.7 Practice

Identify the highlighted word's part of speech:

1. She gave a **clear** answer.
2. They worked **carefully**.
3. We stayed home **because** it rained.
4. The bag is **under** the chair.
5. **Wow!** That was impressive.

**Answers:** 1. adjective, 2. adverb, 3. conjunction, 4. preposition, 5. interjection

## 4.8 Quick Revision

Noun names, pronoun replaces, verb acts/states, adjective describes a noun, adverb modifies, preposition relates, conjunction joins and interjection reacts.

## 4.9 MCQs

1. “Honesty” is a/an: a) proper noun b) abstract noun c) verb d) adverb
2. In “She sings beautifully,” beautifully is: a) adjective b) noun c) adverb d) preposition
3. Which joins clauses? a) pronoun b) conjunction c) noun d) interjection

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-c, 3-b
