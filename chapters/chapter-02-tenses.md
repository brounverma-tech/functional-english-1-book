# Chapter 2: Tenses

## 2.1 Learning Objectives

- understand time and aspect in English verbs;
- form all twelve common tense structures;
- select the correct tense from context;
- avoid frequent tense errors.

## 2.2 What Is Tense?

**Tense** shows when an action or state happens. English has three main time divisions - present, past and future. Each time may be simple, continuous, perfect or perfect continuous.

Simple Hinglish: Tense sirf time nahi batata; yeh action ki **condition** bhi batata hai - regular hai, chal raha hai, complete ho chuka hai, ya duration ke saath continue hai.

## 2.3 Present Tenses

| Tense | Structure | Main use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple Present | S + V1(s/es) | habit, fact | She reads daily. |
| Present Continuous | S + am/is/are + V-ing | action now | She is reading now. |
| Present Perfect | S + has/have + V3 | completed with present link | She has read the chapter. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | S + has/have been + V-ing | duration till now | She has been reading for an hour. |

### 2.3.1 Simple Present

Use for routines, facts, schedules and permanent situations.

- Water boils at 100°C.
- The class begins at 10 a.m.
- He works in Delhi.

### 2.3.2 Present Continuous

Use for an action happening now, a temporary situation or a planned near future.

- I am writing an email.
- We are staying with relatives this week.
- They are meeting the teacher tomorrow.

Stative verbs such as **know, believe, understand, own, need** usually do not take continuous form.

### 2.3.3 Present Perfect vs Simple Past

- **I visited Jaipur in 2025.** Exact finished past time is given.
- **I have visited Jaipur.** Life experience; exact time is not important.

**Since** gives a starting point: since Monday. **For** gives a duration: for three days.

## 2.4 Past Tenses

| Tense | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Simple Past | S + V2 | I attended the lecture. |
| Past Continuous | S + was/were + V-ing | I was attending the lecture. |
| Past Perfect | S + had + V3 | I had attended it before lunch. |
| Past Perfect Continuous | S + had been + V-ing | I had been waiting for an hour. |

Past continuous background action ko show karta hai; simple past interrupting action ko:

> I was studying when the lights went out.

Past perfect two past actions mein earlier action ko clearly marks karta hai:

> The train had left before we reached the station.

## 2.5 Future Forms

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| will + V1 | instant decision, prediction | I will help you. |
| am/is/are going to + V1 | prior plan, visible evidence | It is going to rain. |
| present continuous | fixed arrangement | I am meeting her at 4. |
| simple present | timetable | The bus leaves at 6. |

Traditional four-form table:

- Future Simple: She will write.
- Future Continuous: She will be writing.
- Future Perfect: She will have written.
- Future Perfect Continuous: She will have been writing for two hours.

## 2.6 Negative and Question Forms

- Simple present: **do/does not + V1**; Do/Does + subject + V1?
- Simple past: **did not + V1**; Did + subject + V1?
- Continuous: place **not** after helping verb; move helping verb before subject for a question.
- Perfect: use **has/have/had not**; move has/have/had before subject.

## 2.7 Time Markers

| Marker | Common tense |
|---|---|
| every day, usually | simple present |
| now, at the moment | present continuous |
| already, yet, just | present perfect |
| yesterday, ago, last week | simple past |
| while | past continuous |
| by tomorrow | future perfect |

Markers clues hain, absolute rules nahi. Meaning always comes first.

## 2.8 Common Mistakes

- ❌ I am knowing him. → ✅ I know him.
- ❌ She has completed it yesterday. → ✅ She completed it yesterday.
- ❌ Did you went there? → ✅ Did you go there?
- ❌ I live here since 2024. → ✅ I have lived here since 2024.
- ❌ He will goes. → ✅ He will go.

## 2.9 Practice

Fill in the correct form:

1. She usually ___ (walk) to college.
2. Listen! Someone ___ (knock) at the door.
3. We ___ already ___ (finish) the assignment.
4. They ___ (visit) Agra last month.
5. By next week, I ___ (complete) the project.

**Answers:** 1. walks, 2. is knocking, 3. have finished, 4. visited, 5. will have completed

## 2.10 Quick Revision

- Simple = fact or whole action.
- Continuous = action in progress.
- Perfect = action completed before a reference point.
- Perfect continuous = duration up to a reference point.

## 2.11 MCQs

1. “She ___ here for two years.”  
   a) lives  b) lived  c) has lived  d) is live
2. “When I called, he ___.”  
   a) slept  b) was sleeping  c) has slept  d) will sleep
3. After **did**, we use:  
   a) V1  b) V2  c) V3  d) V-ing

**Answers:** 1-c, 2-b, 3-a
